Image formats

When should you convert WEBP to JPG?

WEBP is great for the web, but JPG can be easier to open in older apps and share with people using a wider range of devices.

Why WEBP exists

WEBP is an efficient image format designed for the web. It often creates a smaller file than a similar JPG while preserving good visual quality.

When JPG is more useful

Convert a WEBP image to JPG when an app, form, printer, or recipient does not accept WEBP. JPG is one of the most widely supported image formats, especially for photographs.

How to convert it

Open QuickSwitch, choose your WEBP file, select JPG from the format menu, and download the converted image. The conversion happens in your browser.
