About QuickSwitch

Small tools for everyday file problems.

QuickSwitch is a free browser-based utility that helps you change common image and text file formats in a few clicks.

How it works

When you choose a file, QuickSwitch processes it directly in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server, and no account is needed.

Why we made it

File formats should not be a roadblock. We made QuickSwitch for quick, simple conversions—without a complicated install, registration form, or surprise paywall.
